The pendulum swings up from any angle and stays upright. PPL learns this in 40,000 steps.

Key quantity
angular velocity \(\omega\)
Threshold speed
\(\omega^*(\theta)=\sqrt{\tfrac{3g}{l}\,(1-\cos\theta)}\), the speed that just coasts to the top, taken at the next-step angle \(\theta'\)
PrincipleWhenReference
Accumulate energy\(|\theta|>0.41\) rad\(\omega^*(\theta')\) along the rotation
Stay upright\(|\theta|\le 0.41\) rad\(\omega^*(\theta')\) toward the top

At 0.41 rad the largest torque just holds the pendulum against gravity.
